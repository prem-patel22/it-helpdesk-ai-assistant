# Printer Troubleshooting Guide

## Printer not printing

Follow these steps in order:

### Step 1: Check basic things
- Is the printer turned ON? (Look for power light)
- Is there paper in the tray?
- Any error lights blinking?

### Step 2: Restart Print Spooler
1. Press Windows + R
2. Type: services.msc
3. Press Enter
4. Find "Print Spooler" in the list
5. Right-click → Restart

### Step 3: Clear stuck print jobs
1. Open Settings → Printers & scanners
2. Click on your printer
3. Click "Open print queue"
4. Right-click stuck jobs → Cancel

### Step 4: Remove and re-add printer
1. Settings → Printers & scanners
2. Click on printer → Remove
3. Click "Add device" to re-add

## When to contact IT
- Printer shows "Service required"
- Paper jam you can't clear
- Error code 0x00000709
