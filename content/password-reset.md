# Password Reset Guide for Employees

## How to reset your network password

If you forgot your password or need to change it, follow these steps:

1. Open your web browser and go to: https://password.company-portal.com
2. Click on "Forgot Password" link
3. Enter your Employee ID
4. Click "Send Reset Link"
5. Check your email inbox (including spam folder)
6. Click the link in the email
7. Create your new password (minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number)
8. Confirm your new password
9. Click "Reset Password"

## Common issues

**Issue: Didn't receive email**
- Check spam folder
- Wait 5 minutes
- Contact IT helpdesk

**Issue: Link expired**
- Links expire after 24 hours
- Request a new reset link
